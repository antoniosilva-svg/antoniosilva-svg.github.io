See the numbers before sending a proposal

  • Account for materials, working time, overhead and a contingency.
  • Calculate a price from your chosen profit margin.
  • Check the effect of a lower offered price.
  • Print a customer proposal and save or load your work as a local file.

The online demonstration is free. The paid kit packages the calculator for offline use with a getting-started guide and an example. It does not unlock exclusive calculation features.

See Payhip for the current price and purchase terms. No subscription to Orzhov is required. Your calculations are processed locally; saving requires an explicit action. Checkout is handled separately by Payhip.

Is it right for your work?

Best suited to straightforward service quotes where you know your costs and want to examine a price. It is not accounting software and does not determine your tax obligations or guarantee profitability.

Try the free version first. If the Portuguese interface does not suit you, the current kit will not solve that language limitation.
